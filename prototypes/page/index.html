he modern internet has a problem, it connects us like never before. This convenience comes the cost of digital soverency. Today, protecting your online privacy has shifted from a conspiricy-fueled choice to a now uphill battle against systematic surveilance capitalism. Online every click, purchase and search is logged by one or more party, now-a-days the internet is primarily made up of technologies and software owned by large tech companies (Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Meta - to name a few). Your data is compiled and catagorised before being monitized which is done by selling your personal information to hidden, private data brokers and tech conglomerates who use your data to determine the best adverts to show you. We no longer use the internet as a user-driven recreational thing, the internet is everywhere, used by everyone. The internet tracks, profiles and predicts your every move, turning the simple act of an expected right (the right to your own digital freedom & privacy). With each passing day privacy becomes less of a default and more of a luxury that we must now actively and exhaustivly.
